## 🎯 Phase 1: Extended Abstract Sprint (Oct 16 – Oct 30, 2026)
> **Goal:** Produce a rigorous, theoretically sound, and compelling 800-word Extended Abstract with 3–5 keywords to ensure immediate acceptance and reviewer acclaim.

### Stage 1.1: Foundations & Scope Definition (Oct 16 – Oct 18)
- [ ] **Define the Research Problem & Questions:**
  - *Primary Problem:* Transition from proprietary/legacy ASCII/ISCII encodings (e.g., Baraha, Nudi, Shree Lipi font hacks) to universal Unicode standard in Kannada web journalism.
  - *Core Research Questions (RQs):*
    - RQ1: How did Unicode adoption shift Kannada newsrooms from static desktop publishing (DTP) image-based publishing to dynamic, platform-searchable digital journalism?
    - RQ2: In what ways did Unicode enable algorithmic discoverability, SEO indexing, social media virality, and mobile aggregation (Dailyhunt, Google News) for vernacular Kannada news?
    - RQ3: What legacy bottlenecks (font rendering, archival loss of pre-Unicode digital archives, keyboard input standards) still affect regional news workflows?
- [ ] **Determine the Theoretical / Conceptual Framework:**
  - *Infrastructural Turn in Media Studies* (Plantin et al., Star & Bowker): Encoding standards as invisible media infrastructure.
  - *Platformisation & Mediatization of Vernacular Media* (Poell, Nieborg, Duffy): How platform architectures shape vernacular language visibility.
  - *Linguistic Digital Divide & Vernacular Public Spheres* (Neyazi, Udupa).
- [ ] **Establish Empirical Methodology:**
  - *Mixed Methods Approach:*
    - Qualitative: Semi-structured in-depth interviews ($N = 6-8$) with Kannada digital journalists, senior web editors (e.g., Prajavani, Vijaya Karnataka, Vartha Bharati, Kannada Prabha, Oneindia Kannada), and digital archivist/type developers.
    - Digital Archival & Content Analysis: Tracing digital evolution of Kannada news websites from early 2000s web archives (Wayback Machine) to contemporary responsive, Unicode-native CMS.
- [ ] **First Guide Sync Prep:** Compile a 1-page bulleted concept note highlighting RQs, Theory, and Method for Guide alignment.
